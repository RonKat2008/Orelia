## Microscopic Description

Sheets and vague nodules of large, polygonal epithelioid cells with vesicular
nuclei, prominent nucleoli, and abundant eosinophilic cytoplasm, many with
eccentric hyaline (rhabdoid) inclusions. Geographic necrosis is present.
Infiltration of skeletal muscle and fascial planes is seen. No conventional
carcinoma component identified. Complete loss of INI1 expression together with
keratin and EMA positivity, CD34 negativity, and the proximal-type
morphology supports the above diagnosis.

## Comment

Findings are diagnostic of epithelioid sarcoma, proximal-type, a rare and
aggressive soft tissue neoplasm. Given the positive deep margin and high
grade, correlation with multidisciplinary sarcoma tumor board review is
recommended. Additional molecular testing (e.g., SMARCB1 sequencing / copy
number) may be performed on request. Block A4 retained for further studies.
